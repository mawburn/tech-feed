Real calls from published editions. New cases go at the top.

## Anthropic IPO: a landmark left in the back (Nov 12–19)

- **What happened:** Nov 12 put "Anthropic begins IPO roadshow" eighth of nine in Also worth knowing, with no `significance` or `thread`. Nov 13 skipped it; Nov 14–15 ran it as "Anthropic IPO"; the Nov 15 weekly left it out; Nov 16–17 had nothing. Pricing (Nov 18, #3) and first trade (Nov 19, #4) were ranked correctly behind urgent security stories, but the agent never marked them `major` (that was added by hand), and Nov 19's summary listed it fifth.
- **Should have been:** On Nov 12, a `notable` top story opening `thread: "anthropic-ipo-2026"`, naming the expected pricing date. A check every day, with an update whenever something material happened. A weekly theme. Pricing and first trade marked `major` and first or second in `executiveSummary`.
- **Why:** The IPO of one of the most prominent AI companies is exactly what a reader would be embarrassed to miss. Opening the thread on day one makes later milestones expected rather than discovered.

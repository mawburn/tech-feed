Theme 03 // Infrastructure
#1482-C

Enterprise Cloud Repatriation: Realities vs Hype

While raw compute savings range between 30% and 55%, operational overhead from dedicated SRE frequently erodes projected 3-year margins unless workloads possess static predictability.

check_circle Where Repatriation Succeeds

Deterministic batch jobs, persistent vector indices with predictable IOPS, and continuous streaming pipelines with heavy egress costs.

cancel Where It Fails

High-burst seasonal commerce, global low-latency consumer products, and teams under 20 platform engineers.

priority_high Why It Matters

Adopting hybrid multi-tier: Kubernetes on bare metal in 2-3 centers while retaining managed IAM, S3, and serverless edge in AWS or GCP.
